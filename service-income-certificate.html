Income Certificate

Get your income certificate online. Usually issued within 15 working days.

What this is

An income certificate is an official document stating your annual family income — the total earned in a year by everyone in your household, not just you. Scholarships, fee concessions and most welfare schemes ask for it before they will consider an application.

Not sure what annual family income covers? The glossary explains it with an example.

Steps to apply

  1. Sign in to JanSeva AI and pick your language.
  2. Fill in the application form — four short sections.
  3. Upload the three documents listed on the right.
  4. Submit, and track progress from your dashboard.

What you need

  • Aadhaar card
    Any clear photo or scan of both sides.
  • Income proof
    A salary slip, or Form 16 if you have one.
  • Recent photograph
    Passport size, taken within the last six months.
